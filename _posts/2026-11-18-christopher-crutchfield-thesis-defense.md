Join Engineers for Exploration for Christopher Crutchfield’s Ph.D. thesis defense, *Designing for Citizen Science: Fish Length Measurement with Commercial Off-the-Shelf Hardware*.

- **Date:** Thursday, November 19, 2026
- **Time:** 1:00 PM Pacific
- **Location:** Henry Booker Room, Jacobs Hall, UC San Diego
- **Online attendance:** Email [ccrutchf@ucsd.edu](mailto:ccrutchf@ucsd.edu) for the Zoom link.

The defense is open to the public, with both in-person and remote attendance welcome.

Christopher’s research explores how anglers and divers can contribute fish-length measurements using accessible consumer technology. These measurements help researchers understand fish populations, but collecting them through traditional scientific surveys can be costly and difficult to scale.

Through E4E’s FishSense project, his work includes phone-based measurements of fish on deck and underwater measurements using a consumer dive camera and laser. It also explores calibration with everyday objects and automation to reduce manual labeling. REEF volunteers have used FishSense Lite to collect fish-length and species data independently, demonstrating its potential for citizen science.

Christopher’s advisors are Professors Curt Schurgers and Ryan Kastner. His committee also includes Professors Nick Antipa, Nikolay Atanasov, and Brice Semmens.
